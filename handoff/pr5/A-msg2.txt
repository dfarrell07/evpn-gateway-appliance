CORENET-7504: Check frr-metrics in the image smokes and e2e

The nested smoke requires frr-metrics.service and every
frrk8s_scrape_success at 1, also after a router restart. The payload smoke
requires --disable-metrics-auth in the FRR image's frr-metrics.

e2e turns frr_exporter on. network.evpn_gateway_test.monitoring checks on
every router that frr-metrics reports FRR's BGP sessions, accepted EVPN
prefixes and VNIs with their remote VTEP counts, reading both again on a
mismatch because those counts can change between the reads. On the
appliance it checks that stopping the unit frees port 9141, that it
follows router restarts, and that turning it off masks it until it is
turned back on.
