Each commit covers one area and passes `make check` on its own, so the PR can be reviewed one commit at a time:

1. **FRR and EVPN alerts and dashboard.**
   - Six Prometheus alerts with promtool tests, and a dashboard over the frr-k8s exporter series from metallb/frr-k8s#528.
   - `make check` gains `check-monitoring`: rule lint, the promtool tests, the dashboards' data sources, job/instance scope and PromQL, and a check that every `frrk8s_*` name is one metallb/frr-k8s#528 exports (`monitoring/frrk8s-metrics.txt`, read at `9a69b64`).

2. **Appliance, VLAN and tunnel dashboard.**
   - node-exporter scrape status.
   - For each VXLAN, bridge, VLAN and WireGuard device: operstate, carrier, and carrier changes counted per interval without overlap.
   - Traffic, drops and errors for the VXLAN, VLAN and WireGuard devices, but not for bridges, whose counters miss bridged frames.
   - Unit tests check each device filter against the devices the on-prem role creates.
3. **Direct Connect and VPN dashboard and alerts.**
   - Connection state, virtual-interface BGP status and VPN tunnel state from CloudWatch, with a Grafana alert for each going down.
   - Why CloudWatch: DX can end on a customer router, and a VPN backup can keep EVPN BGP up, so FRR metrics cannot see these failures.
   - The checker validates each alert rule's states, expression references, condition and time range, and that its query matches the panel it links to.
4. **Runbooks.**
   - `monitoring/runbooks.md` has a section per alert: what it means, what to check on the appliance or in AWS, and what to do.
   - Every alert's `runbook_url` points at its section.
   - `check-monitoring` fails when an alert has no section or a section has no alert.
