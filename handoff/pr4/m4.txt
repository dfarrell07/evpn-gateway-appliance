CORENET-7504: Probe one overlay path and alert on forwarding loss

BGP and EVPN membership can stay up while VXLAN forwarding fails, so an
optional evpn_monitoring timer pings a configured overlay IP across one
stretch every 30s and writes success and RTT through node-exporter's
textfile collector, now enabled in the image and the non-bootc
container. A local ping error leaves the result stale instead of
reporting loss. EGAEvpnPathProbeFailed fires after 5m of failed fresh
probes and EGAEvpnPathProbeUnavailable after 5m of stale, unreadable or
vanished results; the appliance dashboard shows fresh RTT.

The target must be an IP address, the probe unit is sandboxed, and the
role waits until node-exporter exports a result for the configured
target, so it fails on an image whose node-exporter does not read the
textfile directory or when the probe cannot run against that target.
Clearing the target, turning off node_exporter or running teardown.yml
removes the probe and leaves a marker, labelled for node-exporter's
container, that keeps the unavailable alert quiet. check_dashboards.py
scopes the ega_path_probe_* selectors, and a new check-unit target runs
a unit test of the probe script, recorded in ci-source.md.

The e2e probes the first stretch, drops VXLAN packets with nft while BGP
and the remote VTEP stay up, checks failure and recovery, then clears
the target and checks that the probe is removed. Both alerts get
runbook_url links and sections in monitoring/runbooks.md.
